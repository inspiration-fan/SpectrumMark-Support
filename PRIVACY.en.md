# SpectrumMark Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026

This policy applies to SpectrumMark (数安视频水印) for iPhone and macOS. We are the developer of the app. Contact us at [yifannir@outlook.com](mailto:yifannir@outlook.com).

The app processes videos and images on your device. It has no developer-operated media upload server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or cross-app tracking SDK. A SpectrumMark account is not required.

## Videos and images

The app accesses videos and watermark images you choose through the system photo or file picker. It may copy selected media into local app storage for previews, processing, export and quality comparisons. It does not automatically upload your media or results to the developer.

Saving to Photos, exporting to Files and using the system share sheet happen when you choose those actions. iCloud, file services or sharing destinations you select may transfer files off your device. Their handling depends on the service and your system settings.

## Watermark IDs and keys

Invisible watermarks use the ID you enter and a local key. iPhone keys are kept in the system Keychain. macOS uses Keychain where available and may use a private local app file if Keychain storage is unavailable. The app does not send watermark IDs or keys to the developer.

You can choose to import or back up a key. A backup contains the key itself, and you choose where to save or transfer it. Processing history may contain an ID and a key fingerprint, but not the key itself. Verification requires the matching key.

## Local records and diagnostics

Processing history, exports, quality reports, language, theme, export preferences and free usage counts are stored locally. Background diagnostics record task progress, paused or ended states and errors on the device. They are not automatically sent to the developer.

The free allowance is recorded separately on each device. The app does not automatically synchronize keys, history or free usage counts through a developer server. System or cloud backups may include some local files, depending on system behavior and your settings.

## Photo and file permissions

You decide whether to select photos or files and whether to allow saving exported videos to Photos. Granted photo permissions can be managed in system settings. The app does not request contacts, location, microphone or camera permissions, and does not read advertising identifiers.

## Apple purchases and subscriptions

Pro subscriptions use Apple App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles payment and purchase verification. The app uses product, transaction and subscription entitlement information returned by Apple locally to display purchase options, unlock Pro and restore purchases.

The app does not receive card details or send StoreKit transactions to a developer server. Apple's handling of its data is covered by the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

## When you contact support

If you contact us by email or GitHub issue, we receive the email address or GitHub username, message and attachments you choose to provide. We use them to communicate, investigate problems and handle requests. Support correspondence is retained for the time needed to handle support, except where retention is required by law. Contact the support email to request deletion of support correspondence.

GitHub issues and attachments are public. Use email for private matters and do not publicly post private videos, watermark keys, Apple Account credentials or payment information. Review reports or diagnostics before sharing and remove information you do not wish to send. GitHub processes information associated with visits and issue activity under its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Managing and deleting data

You can remove processing records in the app and delete exported files in Photos or Files. Removing a processing record does not automatically delete an export. On macOS, working files are normally in the user's Library/Application Support/SpectrumMark directory. iPhone working files are in the app container.

Back up keys and reports you still need before clearing local app files or uninstalling. Keychain items are managed by the operating system; uninstalling does not guarantee their deletion. Copies saved through sharing, cloud synchronization or backups must be managed in those services separately.

Because there is no developer account or developer server holding your media, we cannot remotely access, recover or delete your videos, keys or local records. Contact support if you need help managing local data.

## Children and tracking

SpectrumMark is a general-purpose video tool. It does not request age or date of birth and is not intended to collect children's personal information. The app does not display third-party advertising, use personal data for cross-app advertising tracking or sell personal information.

## Updates and contact

If data practices change, we will update this policy and its date and adjust the applicable App Store privacy disclosures.
